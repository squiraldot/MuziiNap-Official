## Features
<details>
<summary>🎵 Music & Discovery</summary>
<br>
  
**Ad-Free YouTube & YouTube Music Playback**  
Play songs from YouTube and YouTube Music with an ad-free listening experience.

**Powerful Music Search**  
Search for songs, videos, albums, and playlists directly from YouTube Music.

**Personalized Quick Picks**  
Discover music tailored to your listening preferences with personalized quick picks.

**Playlists, Liked Songs & Subscriptions**  
Access your playlists, liked songs, and subscriptions after signing in.
***
</details>

<details>
<summary>👤 Account & Personalization</summary>
<br>

**YouTube Music Account Integration**  
Sign in with your YouTube Music account and access your personalized content.

**Multiple Account Support**  
Add multiple YouTube Music accounts and switch between them quickly.

**Backup & Restore**  
Back up your app data and restore it whenever you need.
***
</details>

<details>
<summary>🎧 Playback & Audio</summary>
<br>

**Local Music Playback**  
Play your locally stored songs with full background playback support.

**Playback Controls**  
Adjust tempo, pitch, and playback speed to customize your listening experience.

**Spatial Audio**  
Enjoy a more immersive listening experience with spatial audio support.

**Android Auto Support**  
Enjoy a seamless music experience while driving with Android Auto.
***
</details>

Follow real-time synchronized lyrics as your music plays.

Translate and romanize lyrics to better understand and sing along to your favorite songs.
***
</details>

Cache and download songs for offline playback and enjoy your music without an internet connection.

<h2><img src="https://cdn.simpleicons.org/quicktime/Blue" height="15"> Quick Tips!</h2>

#### If MuziiNap isn't showing in Android Auto?

1. Go to Android Auto's settings and tap multiple times on the version in the bottom to enable developer settings
2. In the three dots menu at the top-right of the screen, click "Developer settings"
3. Enable "Unknown sources"
